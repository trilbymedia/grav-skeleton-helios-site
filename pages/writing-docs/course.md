Most documentation is written by people who know the software too well to see it the way a newcomer does. Nobody sets out to write a page that reads like a list of settings, it just turns out that way when the writer starts from what the software can do instead of from what the reader needs to get done.

This course is four short lessons that turn that around. Each one ends with something to try on a page of your own, so by the end you have reworked a real page rather than read about it.

The first lesson is free. The other three open with a membership.
